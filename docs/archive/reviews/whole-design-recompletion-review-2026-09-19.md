**Disposition:** Fail — implementation frozen.

## Evidence reviewed

  `renewed-preimplementation-traceability-review-2026-09-19.md` claim design closure.
- `m9-release-acceptance-design-review-2026-09-19.md` records UC-06 as excluded

## Finding

The claims cannot all be true at once: an implementation-ready current-profile design
cannot simultaneously leave UC-06 without a concrete boundary and claim M9 acceptance
across UC-01–UC-15. The earlier closure reviews remain useful historical evidence, but
are invalid as implementation authorization.

`whole-design-recompletion-plan-2026-09-19.md` enumerates the required closure work
and verification order. No product implementation may resume until its WD-1 through
WD-6 items are closed and a replacement authorization review passes.
