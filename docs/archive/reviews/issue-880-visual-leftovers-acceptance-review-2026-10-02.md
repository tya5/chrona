Layout owns the plot extent and the edge extension (one `plot` fact and one helper, read by the axis, the closed-day backgrounds, the as-of line and the period band); Scene gained no field (the relation label's visual role changed from `annotation` to the existing `text`); View and Domain are untouched; the Theme gained content only. Corpus output was treated as evidence against the general rules, not as an oracle: no corpus data was edited to pass a render criterion (the two byte-identical mirrors and `wallboard` are Theme files, and the one derived Theme was re-pinned by identity). Both rows are `met`; there is no narrowed or deferred row, so no row needs a successor. The two successors below were found on the way and are not acceptance rows.

Disclosures:

- **A project with no calendar shades every day as closed** (found on the `chrona init` starter: 78 `calendar-closed` Rects, weekdays included). Not changed here; filed as [#893](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/893). The starter plot is therefore still entirely shaded.
- **Relation labels that were invisible are now visible text** on every slide that selects relation label content (for example `end->start`, `end->at +5wd`). No label moved; placement was already collision-checked. This changes the look of those slides, as intended.
- **Overlay length changed in 26 committed Scenes** (gridlines, closed days, the as-of line now end at the last row). The canvas below the plot is now empty on `04-tvac-slip` and similar slides; the rows were always that short.
- **Rendered output was read** from `resvg` PNGs of the committed SVG (`02-programme-board`, `axis-ticks`) and of the starter; no browser or other renderer was compared.
- **Surface scope.** The extent and edge rules apply to the table-timeline surface; the dependency-network surface has no timeline and is unaffected.
