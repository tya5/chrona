## Programme-level criteria (optional)

The goal sentence "so that the target presets can later be built in YAML" is not claimed here: no builtin preset binds the catalogue, by decision (design D6), and each target still needs the knob issues its README lists.

## Architecture and release conclusion

The catalogue stays in the resource layer: a new theme-asset-source imported by the existing importer, namespaced beside the starter that `presets/library.yaml` pins, with no schema, importer, role or preset change. Findings of the [architecture review](issue-718-target-parts-catalogue-architecture-review-2026-10-02.md) F1 to F9 were carried out: unconsumed entries are labelled with their owning issue in the manifest (C1), a render test binds a catalogue glyph and pattern through the product (C2), and these rows say `met` only for what the evidence shows (C3). Two gaps depth C cannot close are recorded and have owners: frames, corners and stamps have no consuming Theme role ([#584](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/584), [#587](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/587), and [#848](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/848) for artwork behind an annotation container), and a faithful seigaiha needs an occluding pattern primitive ([#849](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/849)).

Wheel budget: the compressed primary wheel is 3,321,173 bytes without the four files and 3,331,888 with them (+10,715), against the 5,000,000 limit (`tools/check_wheel_size.py` PASS; the built wheel lists all four).

Disclosures:

- **The seals' provenance rests on a judgement.** `危` and `記` are monoline skeletons drawn by looking at the character structure; the bundled Noto Sans JP was a visual reference only and no outline was copied. Letterform structure is not protected expression, but that is a judgement, not a licence grant, and it is stated in the design.
- **No finished preset uses a part.** The renders were made with a scratch Theme over the Controller-Z example; how a part looks inside a tuned preset is untested until a preset binds it. The lantern is legible but small at the gate size of that example.
- **The gallery PNG may differ byte for byte under another rasterizer version.** It is committed evidence from the pinned resvg route, not a derived-evidence manifest path.
- **Ownership is by proposal.** `bulb`, `panel-corner`, `hazard-tab` and the seals name #584 or #587 as their owners because those issues' proposals already name a catalogue glyph; they do not yet specify the role, so the ownership is by proposal, not by merged design.

Exact review-bearing-main three-OS CI and newest-Python materializer run must pass before closing #718; record that run in the issue closing comment.
