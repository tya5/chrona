HALCYON-1 Programme Board, in one bit

The programme board on a 1984 desktop: black and white only, every grey made by dithering, a striped title bar, a menu bar with the date, and each note as an alert box with an OK button. Proof that a Theme can carry the whole chart without a single colour, which is what a print or e-ink preset needs.
