Programme board.
Grid, type, one red.

HALCYON-1's programme board in the International Typographic Style: a strict column grid, one sans-serif family, black on white, and a single red for what is late and for today. No ornament; hierarchy comes only from size, weight and position. Notes are numbered at the mark and set in a numbered column. The most directly usable of the targets.

Hand-drawn direction mock, not chrona output · 1600 × 900
