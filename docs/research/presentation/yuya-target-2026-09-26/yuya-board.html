The Programme Board, Posted at the Bathhouse

HALCYON-1's programme board as the notice board of an old bathhouse on the far side of the river: a lacquered plaque for the title, hanging wooden tags for the six teams, months in kanji, lanterns for milestones, a paper lantern for tonight, a sea of waves for the launch window, and hanging scrolls for the notes.
