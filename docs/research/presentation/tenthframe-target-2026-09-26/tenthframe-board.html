The Programme Board, Scored on League Night

HALCYON-1's programme board as a bowling score sheet in a Buffalo winter: the header is a form, the months are frames, the six teams bowl on six lanes, milestones are pins, today is the foul line, the launch window is the tenth frame, and the notes come out of a photo booth.
