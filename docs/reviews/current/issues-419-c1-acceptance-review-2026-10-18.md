## Honest limits

- The reasons on the 51 single-role rows and the 11 unpainted rows are a **first-pass classification awaiting reviewer audit**. Passing the check proves each purpose is classified and that the classification matches the derived count; it does not prove that each "one role is intended" reason is right.
- Three rows are classified **gap**, not intentional: `member-label` (inside planned/actual/scenario/snapshot labels are registered but every committed label uses the shared `text` role), `label-chip` (finish-delta-chip and member-label-chip registered, never drawn) and `observation-cell` (variance-behind registered, no committed Scene draws an observation cell behind plan). They are reported, not fixed, here.
- 51 of 72 is a count of purposes with one realized role, which is what the issue asks for; it is not a claim that 21 multi-role purposes are all correct.
