## Architecture and release conclusion

Theme declares, Layout measures and stamps (the only layer with font metrics), Scene carries completed facts, the SVG adapter serialises without measuring, PNG and PDF choose the raw serialisation, Typst and TikZ are untouched and warn. Schema change is an in-place optional addition to both live Theme schemas and `scene-v0.7` (Specification 56 section 3.2); the S0 gate passes.

Disclosures:

- **Decision D3** (`box-follows-text` rejects every combination it cannot follow instead of degrading): reverse by removing the mode from the enum.
- **Decision D4** (PNG and PDF take the raw SVG): reverse by passing the fitted SVG through (`V05SvgRenderer()` in `registry.py`).
- **Row 2 and row 9 are narrowed** to #1096 (table cells and the other box families) and **row 6** to the reviewer's adoption in #987; this work did not edit target B.
- A viewer-side limit stays: `text-follows-box` condenses or spaces a line to its measured width, which is the owner's chosen trade (fonts are not embedded); `box-follows-text` needs a viewer that honours SVG filters.

Exact review-bearing-main three-OS CI must pass before closing #1050; that run is recorded in the closing comment.
