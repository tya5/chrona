# Acceptance review — pattern on the group, group-header and row bands (#1282)

Implementation: [#1329](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/pull/1329) (merge `2e579475`), checked against `origin/main` `289444de`. A catalogue `pattern` is admitted on `group-band`, `group-header-band` and `row-band`, each always one Rect, as on `period-band`: the ink is the role's `stroke`, the fill is the substrate, and the pattern is clipped to the band's own extent.
