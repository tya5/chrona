## Programme-level criteria (optional)

[#982](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/982) (open) covers a compile-and-measure test for the Typst source, but only for the compressed-text placement (`#place(left:, top:)`), a verified TikZ `xscale` and the PDF route; it does not say that the whole default output, `#curve` symbols and arrowheads included, must compile on Typst and Tectonic. Recommended successor (to be opened by the owner or reviewer, not by this review): "Compile the Typst and TikZ output of the bundled default and each exportable preset in CI (typst, tectonic) and view the result", possibly as an extra row on #982. Until then the claim "the default and 6 catalogue presets export" means "the adapters emit text", not "the file compiles and looks right"; and the Typst version in the descriptor (`0.13.1`) is a recorded pin, while #982 measured that Typst 0.15 rejects the emitted `place(left:, top:)`.
