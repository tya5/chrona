The allocator accepts measured, renderer-neutral candidates and returns deterministic group-local lane and label-placement results. ## Architecture and release disposition

The ownership boundary is consistent with Specifications 06, 08, 38 §3.1, and 50 and the approved lane/route corrections: Layout owns measured footprints, deterministic placement, lane extent, and later route outcomes; this kernel owns only its generic allocation step. Projection continues to supply selected item and group intent. Scene receives completed geometry and cause records, and adapters serialize them. The module's staged status accurately marks the isolated L3a publication boundary. L3b must integrate it through the composer and remove its staged-module entry; L3c owns public View/preset/slide activation after the integration gates and Editorial/default decision are resolved.

The public-materializer check and byte comparison characterize the existing automatic-mode corpus only. They do not demonstrate lane geometry or rendered name/delta/route acceptance. CI passed, but no issue is ready to close. All nine literal criteria remain deferred to the successor L3 gates.
