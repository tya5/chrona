## Architecture conclusion

View declares table identity and annotation candidates; Layout owns measured lane growth, placement, and typed suppression attribution; Scene carries completed labels; adapters serialize them. The R2 corrections keep full-band search scoped to `fill` profiles, preserve `pack` and non-lane behavior, and introduce no Scene/schema repair, consistent with Specifications 38/50 and the published [R2 architecture reviews](issue-504-fill-only-label-search-architecture-review-2026-09-29.md) and [annotation fallback review](issue-504-annotation-fallback-architecture-review-2026-09-29.md).
