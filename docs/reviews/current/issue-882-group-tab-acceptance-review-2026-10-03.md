## Architecture and release conclusion

The Theme names the tab (lengths, position, pattern, paint and order); Layout owns its geometry, the header text offset and the bound on the text; Scene carries the completed Rect and its pattern; adapters decide nothing. View is untouched. Theme v0.11 and v0.13 gained optional properties in place; the S0 gate classifies them as additive without an expected-delta entry, and reported no unused or inapplicable entry of other PRs as a failure.

Defaults leave output unchanged: a Theme without the role, with an empty role or with treatment `none` draws today's header (an explicit `none` adds only the usual `absent` decoration disposition), and the 40 existing public slides regenerated byte-identical on the final tree; that is no-change evidence, not a quality bar.

Adapters: SVG and PNG draw the tab with or without a pattern. Typst and TikZ draw a solid tab as a plain Rect (asserted line by line) and reject a patterned tab, with the whole scene, as `E_VISUAL_CAPABILITY_UNSUPPORTED`, the same rule as every patterned Rect; no adapter code changed.

Contrast: the tab is a `DECORATION` Rect gated on the band or canvas under it, a patterned tab on substrate, ink on substrate and ink on ground. The header text never lies on the tab (offset or bounded) and stays gated on its band under the #884 `groupHeader` class; a Scene with a tab drawn under the text is judged on the tab's substrate and ink (tested on Scene documents). The corpus contrast report has 0 errors and a `group-tab` row. Two first drafts of the evidence Theme failed the gate (a stripe ink equal to the canvas colour, then bands and closed days on a dark Scheme) and were corrected in that Theme with no rule relaxed.

Disclosures:

- **An `end` tab stands at the far edge of the header bounds,** which span table and timeline, so it can lie outside a band whose extent is `table`; `start` is the Title Card position.
- **Header text beside a tab is shortened** with an ellipsis and a warning when it does not fit; a header without a tab is unchanged and unbounded, as before.
- **Whether the pattern's ink-on-host pair should apply to a decoration whose opaque substrate covers its host** is a question for the contrast lane (#884, #970), not changed here.
- **The evidence slide lives in Controller Z,** not on the HALCYON-1 board (see row 7).
- **Rendered output was read** from resvg PNGs; no browser or other renderer was compared.

Exact review-bearing-main three-OS CI and the newest-Python materializer run must pass before closing #882; that run is recorded in the closing comment.
