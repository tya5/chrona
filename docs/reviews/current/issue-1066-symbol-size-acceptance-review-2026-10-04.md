## Architecture and release conclusion

Theme declares two optional number tokens per mark role (additive in `theme-v0.11` and `theme-v0.13`; `schema_equivalence --base-rev origin/main` PASS, no expected-delta entry needed for additive properties); Layout owns the symbol geometry (`MarkGeometry.symbol_extent`, `MarkBandFrame.symbol_bounds`, point marks in `compose_item_marks`); Scene and adapters are unchanged. Lanes, folded header points and the lane footprints compose through the same frame and so honour the tokens without a second path. Seven of eight mutations fail a test; the eighth (a containment check) was redundant with the pair validation and was removed.
