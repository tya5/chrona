## Architecture and release conclusion

Theme declares; Layout owns the texture's region, tile phase and `canvas` pseudo-slot; Scene owns the glow region and the omission ladder and emits the texture as an ordinary patterned Rect; adapters serialize completed values and decide nothing. The texture needed no Scene schema change; the glow added an optional `paint.glow` to Scene v0.7 and three role properties plus a binding key to Theme v0.11 and v0.13, in place (Specification 56 section 3.2); the S0 gate `python -m tools.schema_equivalence --base-rev origin/main` passed on both slices. Corpus data, presets, catalogues and #582, #583 and #718 files were not edited. No row is `met` in full and none is `not met`: the issue allows splitting per treatment, and each undelivered treatment has its own issue.

Disclosures:

- **Texture hides a canvas gradient.** The substrate is opaque and paints over the canvas fill; a transparent, overlay or seeded texture (Montmartre grain, Off-World rain) is [#891](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/891).
- **Typst, TikZ and PDF reject a texture** like any pattern, and never receive a glow (their profiles do not admit it).
- **A role that names a glow cannot be the shared `text` role,** the canvas or an Icon role; a title glow is therefore not expressible yet.
- **A double halo** is intentionally brighter than a drop shadow of the same parameters; the Theme owns the strength through opacity and blur.
- **Rendered output was read** from resvg PNGs of the two new slides and of probe renders; no browser or other renderer was compared.
- **Two small product fixes surfaced** and are in #877: `tools/presentation_coverage.py` accepts Scene v0.7, and a Theme catalogue pattern or glyph counts as a read closure input.

Exact review-bearing-main three-OS CI and the newest-Python materializer run must pass before closing #587; that run is recorded in the closing comment.
