## Architecture and release conclusion

Theme declares the cone; Layout completes its polygon beside the as-of line; Scene completes the gradient (with the stop opacity this issue added to the completed `LinearGradient`) and the omission ladder; the SVG adapter serializes `stop-opacity` and decides nothing; the gates read the completed Scene only. Scene v0.7, `theme-v0.11` and `theme-v0.13` gained optional members in place (Specification 56 section 3.2). The S0 gate `python -m tools.schema_equivalence --base-rev origin/main` passed on the code PR, with the Scene and Theme additions additive. Against a later main it reports the chained #585 `values` entries as "does not apply" (tracked in #970; not this work's, untouched). Corpus data, presets and catalogues were not edited; the #585, #893, #889 and #882 files were not touched.

Disclosures:

- **The cone is ground, not content:** no contrast class and no floor of its own; a decoration is not judged against it.
- **A required cone fails under the default baseline profile** (`gradientFidelity` absent means `required`, as for every treatment); a Theme that wants the baseline to omit it declares `decorative-optional`.
- **One cone per as-of marker**; the composition of several cones is not defined (a surface has one as-of marker).
- **Rendered output was read** from resvg PNGs of the slide and the synthetic render; no browser or other renderer was compared.

Exact review-bearing-main three-OS CI and the newest-Python materializer run must pass before closing #890; that run is recorded in the closing comment.
