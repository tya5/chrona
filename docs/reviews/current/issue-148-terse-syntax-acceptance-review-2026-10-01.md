## Architecture and release conclusion

A leaf front end: `terse` imports only `core`, the CLI adapter dispatches on suffix for four commands, and there is no schema, Scene, presentation, storage or Store change and no derived evidence change. The only narrowed row is 3, where the hand-off composition shipped and the overlay is [#806](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/806); row 14 is a separate Project-model issue, [#788](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/788). Both successors are open, and every row is met or has an explicit successor.

Disclosures:

- **`chrona validate` passes a dependency cycle** that `schedule` and `render` reject ([#780](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/780), open, found in the #142 design review); a plan inherits this.
- **No rendered artifact is inspected here.** No criterion concerns appearance; byte equality of plan render against YAML render is the evidence for row 7.
