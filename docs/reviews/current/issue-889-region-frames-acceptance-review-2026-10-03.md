## Architecture and release conclusion

Layout Profile declares where a frame is; the engine records it with the arranged bounds and never reads it back; Layout completes the Rect, its pseudo-slot, omission and corner reduction; Theme declares one role that paints it; Scene emits it first after the canvas texture and completes its paint like any Rect; adapters serialize. The Layout Profile v0.10 and Theme v0.11 and v0.13 additions are optional and in place (Specification 56 section 3.2). No Scene schema change (a patterned frame makes the Scene v0.7, as for any patterned Rect). Corpus data, presets, catalogues and #585, #893, #884 and #950 files were not edited.

Disclosures:

- **A panel is the node's allocation, not the plot:** under a timeline slot taller than its rows the panel keeps the empty strip; the plot overlays still end at the last row.
- **A role shared by every frame** paints them alike; per-frame variants, a frame removal syntax for overrides, a plot-fitted panel and corner glyphs are additive extensions recorded in the work record.
- **A patterned (halftone) frame has no separate outline:** the pattern closure rule forbids `strokeWidth` on a patterned role.
- **The corner is a pixel radius** (`frameCornerRadius`), reduced to half the shorter side with `W_LAYOUT_REGION_FRAME_CORNER_REDUCED`, not the #491 ratio (owner decision F6).
- **The dependency-network surface ignores `frame`;** Typst and TikZ draw a plain frame and reject a patterned one; PNG is the SVG through resvg.
- **A wobble or glow reaches slightly past a frame's stroke extent,** as for every primitive.

Exact review-bearing-main three-OS CI and the newest-Python materializer run must pass before closing #889; that run is recorded in the closing comment.
