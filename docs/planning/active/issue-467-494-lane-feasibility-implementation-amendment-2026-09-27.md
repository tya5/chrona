## Cross-slice stop and return-to-design conditions

Pause the affected slice and publish a design correction, architecture re-review and amended plan before continuing if any of these occur:

- the 4wd lag does not produce May 3–May 17 through the ordinary scheduler, unexpectedly changes actual or frozen-baseline facts, or changes unexamined copies/contexts;
- the 02 lane count exceeds 12, the named chain cannot share a lane, any packed name/delta is suppressed or crosses required geometry, or the third row requires a project-specific branch or further unreviewed ladder rung;
- a remaining route cause cannot be measured per relation, a supposed quality rejection is actually an egress collision, any required-label crossing occurs, or a required Scene/diagnostic schema change is discovered;
- automatic mode changes beyond independently approved behavior, a packaged preset cannot materialize, default migration causes regressions, or a public adapter produces geometry inconsistent with Layout;
- the then-published #498 readable Editorial default cannot satisfy its bare-render row-guide/name-disposition and appearance contracts under lanes; reconcile that cross-design conflict before changing its resource rather than exempting it silently;
- #480/#481/#486/#487/#488 or #466 phase ownership cannot be satisfied within the selected View/Project/Layout/Scene/adapter boundaries;
- any one of the literal criteria proves incompatible with measured output. Do not narrow, reinterpret, or defer acceptance inside implementation.

## Publication discipline

Each L1, L2, and L3 unit is reviewable and publishable separately, with its own exact staged diff, generated-output batch, focused evidence and CI state. Before each push fetch `origin/main`, compare ahead/behind and target commits, inspect staged resources/generated files, and publish serially. Reconcile unexpected remote changes instead of overwriting them. After each push confirm the remote commit and CI state; identify that published SHA as the next slice's base. Keep the acceptance review as a separate final publication. Do not close reviewer-maintained boards or close issues on unpushed or Scene-only evidence.
