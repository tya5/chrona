# Design Correction — Owner-amended lane-count acceptance (#467, #494)

**Plan:** [acceptance rebaseline design plan](../planning/active/issue-467-494-l3-lane-count-acceptance-rebaseline-design-plan-2026-09-27.md). **Corrects:** numeric feasibility and compulsory-third-row implications in the [lane feasibility/route correction](issue-467-494-lane-feasibility-route-correction-2026-09-27.md), [miter-envelope amendment](issue-467-l3b-b1b-2-miter-envelope-amendment-2026-09-27.md), and their successor L3 plans. **Source:** the 2026-09-27 owner amendment to [#467](https://github.com/tya5/chrona/issues/467). Earlier documents retain their historical evidence; this correction supersedes their twelve-lane gate.

## Selected behavior and evidence identity

The public 02 acceptance gate is **at most fourteen lanes** for the same twenty-six selected root/attached items. The `structure → avionics → bus-test` chain must occupy one lane. This is a release condition, not a target to optimize by suppressing text, reducing the visible footprint, weakening the collision rules or excluding countable items. The final acceptance review reports the exact lane count and a source-keyed lane-membership inventory. For each lane beyond ten in the group-ordered, stable final lane sequence, record the lane ID, representative/member IDs, and the measured reason why those items cannot join a preceding compatible lane. Allowed reasons are mark collision, required-label collision, or chain rule; multiple reasons are reported when present. The report derives from the completed Layout plan and its conflict evidence, not visual guesswork. An unexplained eleventh-or-later lane fails acceptance even if the total is fourteen or fewer.

The owner amendment does **not** relax required-name and selected-delta visibility, deterministic insertion, exact `automatic` regression, default/preset migration, or three reproducible lane slides. It does not relax #494: zero `egress-collision` suppressions on 02, measured causes for all other suppressions, no route crossing required labels on 02/11/12, and attributed 02 membership changes. The current 4wd Project schedule remains authoritative; the WIP 13-lane report is an early feasibility signal only.

## Label ladder and route tradeoff

The bounded third stagger row remains an **available** generic Layout candidate after rows one and two, with existing stable start/end order and measured block extent. It is no longer a condition for satisfying the lane-count gate, and a design or implementation review must not force its use merely to reduce a thirteen- or fourteen-lane composition. If it is used by the deterministic candidate search, its required text still enters phase-one obstacles and all route/canvas checks. The final rendered review must compare count, readability and route-cause effects; if a selected third-row placement causes unacceptable suppression, return to Layout design for a generic, route-aware disposition rather than inserting target-specific exceptions or removing required-label obstacles. This correction does not introduce a new label-placement algorithm, fourth rung, route exemption or quality-bound change.

## Responsibility, failure and migration

Project/Schedule keep selected dates; View selects lane mode and text intent; Theme provides geometry and typography; Layout measures marks/labels, computes source-keyed footprints, selects candidates, closes one lane plan, and records conflict/route causes. Scene projects completed geometry and diagnostics; adapters serialize. The acceptance inventory is derived from Layout identities and measured conflicts, so neither Scene nor SVG scraping chooses causes. The conservative 10× path envelope remains intact and target-independent. No public schema change follows from a numeric issue gate. Published design/specification text and active implementation plans must be amended before threshold-dependent code, while prior acceptance reviews remain historical and may be annotated by successor review. At final activation, regenerate the affected Scene/SVG/PNG/materializer corpus as one batch and inspect rendered output.

## Decision boundary

S2b source-keyed facet mapping has no hard-coded ceiling and proceeds independently. B2/L3b is the first current-main composition slice that can establish the fourteen-lane gate and lane-above-ten causes. B3 route evidence and L3c activation remain separate gates. If B2 returns more than fourteen, fails the named chain or cannot attribute extra lanes, pause the affected slice and correct the design. A WIP count alone cannot waive these gates.
