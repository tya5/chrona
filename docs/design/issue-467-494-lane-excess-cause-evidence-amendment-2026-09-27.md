# Design Amendment — Source-keyed excess-lane cause evidence (#467, #494)

**Corrects:** the ambiguous global-ordinal inventory wording in the [owner-amendment correction](issue-467-494-lane-count-acceptance-correction-2026-09-27.md). **Plan:** [acceptance rebaseline](../planning/active/issue-467-494-l3-lane-count-acceptance-rebaseline-design-plan-2026-09-27.md). **Public base:** `e6d50c3c9d12facba916727a041dea7fbe2de526`. This is a design correction discovered before B2 product code, not a relaxation of #467's literal criterion.

## Why the earlier wording is insufficient

The issue's ten-lane estimate is a **sum of per-group first-fit counts**: Spacecraft bus 3, Imaging payload 2, Assembly/integration/test 1, Ground segment 2, Launch/range 1, Mission operations 1. Calling the global eleventh lane an "excess" is wrong: it may be the first mandatory lane of a later group, where group isolation, not a mark/label collision or chain rule, is the reason. The current `LaneAllocationResult` records only successful assignments. `_Lane.try_place` discards the first collision and all failed ladder candidates, so its output cannot prove the owner-requested attribution.

## Selected evidence model

The acceptance inventory compares **each group's final lane count** with that group's published mark-only estimate above, in canonical group order. For a group with final count over its estimate, list every lane beginning at that group's `estimate + 1` ordinal, even if another group is below estimate and the sum of positive differences exceeds `total - 10`. This conservative inventory covers every net lane above ten without misclassifying a group's first lane. Record final total and all six per-group counts; if group identity differs from the issue's six groups, stop for a data/design disposition rather than inventing a mapping.

For each listed lane, record its stable lane ID, group ID and ordinal, representative/source IDs, and the rejected candidate-to-existing-lane attempts that caused the opening. Layout must retain immutable, source-keyed rejection evidence from its deterministic search. Each attempt names the candidate and candidate member(s), attempted lane, blocker member/facet/required-label identity where available, and **all** relevant causes: `mark-collision`, `required-label-collision`, or an explicitly enforced `chain-rule`. A mark/label cause identifies measured intersecting footprints and clearance; required-label rejection records failed ladder levels, not merely the selected result. Evidence records reflect the exact seed frame, Theme/font identity, scale and completed geometry used by the final immutable lane plan. No Scene/SVG reverse-engineering or second allocation pass is allowed.

Predecessor preference alone is not an enforced chain rule and must **not** be labeled `chain-rule`. The named chain remains a separate hard placement check. If a policy later prohibits an otherwise fitting lane to preserve a chain, it needs an approved generic rule and a typed relation-ID witness before that cause can be used. If an extra lane opens solely because of canvas bounds, invalid metrics or other unclassified input, the accepted three-way taxonomy cannot explain it; fail the above-ten evidence gate and return to design instead of mislabeling it. Multiple valid causes may be reported together. Root/attached items count once; comparison/Actual facets only provide their source-keyed mark witnesses.

## Ownership and migration

The Layout allocator returns successful assignments **and** immutable rejection/opening evidence; the one-plan composer carries both without recomputation. The final acceptance review formats that evidence against the six group estimates. View, Scene and adapters do not classify geometry. This adds an internal typed result contract and focused tests, not a View/Scene schema or user-visible rendering change. Existing automatic bytes remain unchanged. B2 must publish this evidence model and its focused tests before claiming the amended numeric gate; S2b remains independent.
