# Design Correction — Annotation Connector Topology (#466)

**Predecessors:** [general placement design](issue-466-general-placement-design-2026-09-26.md), [route-priority correction](issue-466-general-placement-route-priority-correction-2026-09-26.md), and [topology design plan](../planning/active/issue-466-annotation-route-topology-design-plan-2026-09-26.md). The [perimeter escape](issue-466-general-placement-bounded-escape-correction-2026-09-26.md) remains rejected. This document resolves the O2 connector policy; it does not approve the later View candidate grammar, nearest-free search, or Theme balloon contract.

## Evidence and use cases

The published `controller-z/annotations` rail leaders use local routes. In the unpublished shared-index experiment, the `firmware-slip` leader instead falls back to a direct diagonal and the later `bringup-risk` leader reaches its box only by travelling near the slide's top, left, and bottom edges. Its box is at approximately `(24, 1131)`, while its mark is near `(1221, 317)`; the accepted route visits `(1103, 26)`, `(22, 26)`, and `(22, 1153)`. A larger search cap found the same topology. The issue is not solved by a faster search.

Required visual obstacles divide a source mark from a distant rail box. A route that must never cross any prior route can be forced around the entire surface even when a short, comprehensible rail leader exists. Excluding all routes from the inventory would conceal the crossing and defeat the shared-obstacle contract. A direct diagonal fallback also discards the Layout profile's orthogonal connector intent. For in-plot balloons, by contrast, a candidate with an anchor and box in the same free component must remain strictly clear of dependency and as-of strokes.

## Selected topology rule

Layout evaluates **box and connector as one candidate**. It first searches finite box positions and endpoint ports, then tests the full connector (including egress and attachment) against the one surface index. A box position is accepted as a fit only when both the box and its connector satisfy the candidate's collision and route-quality policy. The selected pair is committed atomically; a rejected pair leaves no obstacle in the index. A candidate may use more than one finite connector topology, ordered by declared policy. Its search count includes each tested box/port/topology tuple, and its decision records the selected topology and collision IDs.

The strict topology forbids crossing all declared obstacles. A **bridged orthogonal topology** is available only where the connector policy explicitly permits it (the legacy rail leader preset may select it after strict local search). It still forbids marks, text, annotation boxes, ports except named endpoints, and rule barriers. It may cross a prior leader or semantic dependency stroke transversely at no more than eight isolated points. It may not overlap a stroke collinearly, run along one, cross a junction or endpoint, or cross an as-of line. The local corridor is the bounding rectangle of the source mark and candidate box, inflated on each side by half the measured box width (at least two route clearances), clipped to the content bounds. This admits small lateral detours while excluding a journey around the slide perimeter. The route must satisfy the existing bend and detour limits. Crossing count precedes length and bend count in the stable cost order. If no legal local topology fits, #449's visible route fallback remains explicit and diagnosed; it is not reported as a collision-free fit.

For each permitted crossing, Layout computes a finite gap in the **annotation connector**, centered on the crossing and wider than the crossed stroke. The semantic dependency or earlier leader remains continuous. Layout emits one connector placement with the original source/target identity and a path-command sequence containing multiple `move` segments; the gap is completed geometry, not an SVG-only paint trick. Scene projects those commands and both SVG and typeset adapters serialize them without routing or choosing a gap. O2 derives gap length from the resolved connector and crossed-stroke widths plus two clearances, so it adds no hidden renderer default or new Theme resource key. A later Theme treatment may expose a controlled gap token. No bridge is available for a `tail` connector: plot balloons must find a collision-free box/connector pair or take the next candidate.

## Ordering and failure

The inventory is monotone: required geometry, semantic routes, optional labels, then annotations in stable View order. During an annotation search, candidate box and connector are provisional. Once the pair is selected, Layout adds the box, text/visuals, any optional note index, and the final visible connector segments. A bridge gap does not erase the crossed obstacle and does not license a later box to cover it. Annotation purpose does not change placement search; connector capability and policy are selected by the normalized candidate. A suppressed optional note index never suppresses its independent leader.

Candidate search is bounded by region, box steps, endpoint count, route states, and crossing count. The legacy rail box sequence is the anchor-aligned position followed by alternating one-box-height steps, at most `2 * floor(rail_height / box_height) + 1` tested boxes; each box tries at most four mark ports, strict before bridged topology, with at most 1024 route states per topology and eight bridge crossings. These bounds and the actual counts are recorded in the decision. Exhaustion is distinguishable from no geometric fit. The first declared candidate is completed visibly if no candidate fits and no explicit suppress outcome applies; visible fallback identifies its crossed obstacle IDs and is never silently called a fit. No global exemption for a row, mark, text family, dependency, or prior leader is allowed.

## Migration and proof

This correction may intentionally change legacy rail leader bytes in O2. The later **rung-to-candidate normalization itself** must be byte-identical against the accepted O2 public baseline. A connector bridge is a Layout/Scene treatment change, not a View schema change. Before O2 acceptance, compare all public Scene/SVG materializers and rendered images in one batch; reject outer-perimeter routes, lost leaders, undeclared crossings, or newly suppressed semantic dependencies. Add neutral tests for a note beside a dependency, an unavoidable rail crossing with a visible bridge, a strict in-plot no-crossing route, bounded search, and an explicit visible fallback.

The exact candidate-resource schema and tail/balloon Theme schema still require their own design completion and whole-architecture review before implementation.
